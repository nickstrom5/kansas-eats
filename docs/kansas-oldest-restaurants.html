Kansas's oldest restaurants

Trail taverns, slider counters and chicken dinner halls that have fed Kansas since 1960 or before: 7 places in all, oldest first. The oldest, Hays House Restaurant in Council Grove, goes back to 1857.

Which year counts

We count from the year a place opened as it stands today, going by a dated source that ties the year to it. Claims stay claims: the Hays House calls itself the oldest restaurant west of the Mississippi, and Tasting Table (September 2025) names it Kansas's oldest, open since 1857. A building's age isn't the business's, and a restaurant that moved counts from the year it reached its current address.

Oldest first

  1. Hays House Restaurant

    112 W Main St, Council Grove

    Heritage table Since 1857

    On the Santa Fe Trail in Council Grove since 1857; one of the 8 Wonders of Kansas Cuisine.

    Tasting Table's oldest restaurant in every state (Sept. 2025): Kansas's is the Hays House, open since 1857 ("oldest west of the Mississippi" is its own claim).

  2. The Cozy Inn

    108 N 7th St, Salina

    Sliders & drive-ins Since 1922

    Sliders on North 7th Street since spring 1922.

    The Kansas Sampler Foundation's 8 Wonders page: opened in the spring of 1922.

  3. NuWAY Burgers

    1416 W Douglas Ave, Wichita

    Sliders & drive-ins Since 1930

    Loose-meat burgers on West Douglas since 1930.

    The Kansas Sampler Foundation's 8 Wonders page: the NuWay tradition began on July 4, 1930, and the original is at 1416 W. Douglas; Visit Wichita (July 2026) lists it open.

  4. Old Mill Tasty Shop

    604 E Douglas Ave, Wichita

    Sliders & drive-ins Since 1932

    A soda fountain since 1932.

    Visit Wichita (July 2026): since 1932.

  5. Chicken Annie's Original

    1143 E 600th Ave, Pittsburg

    Crawford County chicken Since 1934

    Annie Pichler's chicken dinners, northeast of Pittsburg, since 1934.

    Kansas Tourism (Aug. 2026): founded 1934.

  6. Chicken Mary's

    1133 E 600th Ave, Pittsburg

    Crawford County chicken Since 1942

    Next door to Chicken Annie's Original on East 600th Avenue since 1942.

    Kansas Tourism (Aug. 2026): established 1942.

  7. Gebhardt's Chicken Dinners

    124 N 260th St, Mulberry

    Crawford County chicken Since 1946

    A farmhouse chicken-dinner hall near Mulberry, open since 1946.

    Kansas Tourism (Aug. 2026): opened 1946.
