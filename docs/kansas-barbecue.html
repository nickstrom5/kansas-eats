The Kansas side and beyond

Visit KC's barbecue directory lists Joe's Kansas City Bar-B-Que on West 47th Avenue, Slaps BBQ in Strawberry Hill, Jack Stack in Overland Park and Lenexa, Gates in Leawood, Meat Mitch and Q39 South on the Kansas side. Kansas Tourism's road-trip list (May 2025) adds Guy & Mae's Tavern in Williamsburg, an 8 Wonders of Kansas Cuisine pick, and pits from Marysville to Ellsworth; Visit Wichita (July 2026) adds Wichita's own.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Wichita (6), Leawood (2), Overland Park (2), Kansas City (2), Marysville (1), Emporia (1), Hill City (1), Williamsburg (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a Kansas tourism office's own page for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

On your phone, Kansas Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
