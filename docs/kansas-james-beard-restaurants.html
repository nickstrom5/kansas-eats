What counts as an honor here

One source, reported as facts rather than scores: the James Beard Foundation. Drastic Measures, a Shawnee cocktail bar, was an Outstanding Bar finalist in 2023 and a semifinalist again in 2026; Of Course in Overland Park and FioRito in Wichita were 2026 Best Chef: Midwest semifinalists; Georges French Bistro, John Brown's Underground, Wild Child, The Restaurant at 1900 and Saltwell Farm Kitchen made the 2025 lists. The Brookville Hotel in Abilene, Kansas's America's Classic of 2007, closed in May 2026. No MICHELIN Guide covers Kansas.
