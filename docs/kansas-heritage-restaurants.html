Heritage tables

4 places where Kansas history is part of dinner: a Santa Fe Trail tavern, a restored church, a Route 66 store and Volga German cooking. Each was checked in fall 2026.

Trail towns and settlers

The Hays House in Council Grove has served the Santa Fe Trail since 1857; Tasting Table (September 2025) names it Kansas's oldest restaurant, and "oldest west of the Mississippi" is its own claim. KMUW (January 2026) points to Gella's in Hays for Volga German dishes like green bean dumpling soup and grebble. KANSAS! magazine (March 2026) found Chef Stan's Place in a restored 1905 church in Strong City, and Kansas Tourism's Route 66 guide (July 2026) stops at Nelson's Old Riverton Store.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Council Grove (1), Strong City (1), Hays (1), Riverton (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or a Kansas tourism office's own page for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
